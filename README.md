# 🎓 University Academic Performance Analytics Dashboard

An enterprise-grade, high-end **Neon Dark Mode** Power BI dashboard designed for cross-faculty academic performance evaluation, graduation tracking, and GPA analytics across **5,275 student records**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This analytics solution provides interactive data visualization for university stakeholders to track student academic achievements across four primary faculties:
* **Faculty of Mathematics and Natural Sciences (FMIPA)**
* **Faculty of Engineering**
* **Faculty of Economics and Business (FEB)**
* **Faculty of Law**

The dashboard leverages custom DAX measures, cascading slicer navigation, and dynamic dataset refreshing from a structured Excel backend.

---

## 🎨 UI/UX & Design Architecture

* **Theme Concept:** Obsidian Dark Mode with High-Contrast Neon Highlights
* **Background Canvas:** Dark Obsidian (`#0B0F19`)
* **Container Cards:** Deep Slate Navy (`#151B29`) with rounded borders (`8px`)
* **Neon Accent Palette:**
  * 🩵 **Cyan (`#00F5FF`):** Average GPA & Very Satisfactory Category
  * 🟢 **Mint Green (`#32FF7E`):** Cumlaude Rate & Top Achievements
  * 🩷 **Magenta (`#FF00FF`):** Total Cumlaude Count & Key Highlights
  * 🟡 **Neon Amber (`#FFB800`):** Faculty Highlights & Satisfactory Category
